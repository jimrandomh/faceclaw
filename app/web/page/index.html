The ring

Faceclaw is driven by four gestures on a ring. Swipe on this pad, or use the buttons.

tap · double-tap · hold · swipe

Scrolling over the display works too. Clicking it is a tap: glasses have no pointer, so in a list it picks the highlighted row, while icons and the app switcher respond to where you click.

Double-tap until the display sleeps, then tap for the Glanceboard.
